🧠 Cognitive Neuroscience & Consciousness Research

Unraveling the Neural Mysteries of Conscious Experience

While you are reading these words, the electrochemical activity of billions of nerve cells is generating a conscious experience of these words: right now. But how do all those interconnected cells in your brain do that? Answering this question is the ultimate intellectual challenge for members of the Conscious Brain Lab.

Our Scientific Architecture

How we combine psychophysics, electrophysiology, neuropharmacology, and computational modeling to dissect consciousness.

Multimodal Investigation of the Mind

We are hosted at the University of Amsterdam (Simon van Gaal) and the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (Johannes Fahrenfort). Our projects probe subjective experience across multiple scales—from interneuron baseline firing rates to large-scale frontoparietal networks.

Combining high-density EEG with decoding toolboxes (such as the lab's open-source ADAM toolbox), pharmacological challenges, and 7T fMRI allows us to disentangle raw perceptual sensitivity from decisional criteria and confidence.
