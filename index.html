🧠 Cognitive Neuroscience & Consciousness Research

Unraveling the Neural Mysteries of Conscious Experience

While you are reading these words, the electrochemical activity of billions of nerve cells is generating a conscious experience of these words: right now. But how do all those interconnected cells in your brain do that? Answering this question is the ultimate intellectual challenge for members of the Conscious Brain Lab.

Core Pillars

How We Investigate the Conscious Mind

We combine state-of-the-art neuroimaging paradigms, computational modeling, and clinical partnerships to decipher how subjective experience emerges.

⚡

Conscious vs Unconscious Processing

Using visual psychophysics, backward masking, continuous flash suppression, and attentional blink to pinpoint the exact temporal and spatial neural signatures of conscious awareness.

High-Density EEG 7T fMRI Psychophysics
🎯

Cognitive Control & Decision Making

Exploring how confidence, perceptual bias, and criterion setting interact with conscious awareness using formal Bayesian computational modeling and signal detection theory.

Bayesian Modeling Metacognition Decision Theory
🏥

Disorders of Consciousness

Applying consciousness paradigms to clinical questions: understanding what happens during sleep, general anesthesia, and in patients with unresponsive wakefulness syndrome at Amsterdam UMC.

Amsterdam UMC Anesthesia Clinical EEG
🧪

Neuropharmacology & Neuromodulation

ERC-funded pharmacological interventions (GABAergic, NMDA, cholinergic agonists) combined with neuroimaging to establish causal neural mechanisms of subjective states.

ERC Grant Pharmacology MEG
🌀

Hypnosis & Cognitive Penetration

Investigating how hypnosis alters sensations and overrides automatic information processing, determining the cortical depth at which top-down control modulates perception.

Top-Down Control Sensory Inhibition
🛠️

Open Science & ADAM Toolbox

Developing and maintaining open-source tools for multivariate EEG/MEG decoding (ADAM Toolbox) and sharing open datasets with the global neuroscience community.

ADAM Toolbox MATLAB / Python Open Data
