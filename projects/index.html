Research Projects & Themes

Our lab investigates the mechanisms of subjective experience, metacognition, and neural information processing across healthy and clinical populations.

🧠

1. How Does the Brain Generate Subjective Experience?

Many of the core projects in our lab are focused on this fundamental question. We use psychophysical techniques—such as backward masking, continuous flash suppression, and the attentional blink—to precisely manipulate conscious perception. Combining these behavioral paradigms with high-density EEG, 7T fMRI, and MEG, we track how conscious experience unfolds dynamically in the human brain.

Attentional Blink Visual Masking 7T fMRI & EEG
🏥

2. Disorders of Consciousness & Clinical Applications

What happens when a person loses consciousness, such as when falling asleep or when placed under general anesthesia? How can we translate basic neuroscience into clinical diagnostics and prognostics for patients with disorders of consciousness (e.g., coma, unresponsive wakefulness syndrome)? These translational projects are carried out in close collaboration with neurologists and anesthesiologists at the Amsterdam University Medical Center (Amsterdam UMC).

Amsterdam UMC General Anesthesia Coma & Prognosis
📊

3. Consciousness, Cognition & Perceptual Decision Making

We explore how conscious awareness influences decision-making and cognitive control. In particular, we apply formal Bayesian computational modeling to disentangle genuine shifts in perceptual sensitivity from criterion/response bias, and examine how these criterion shifts modulate subjective confidence ratings and metacognitive evaluations.

Bayesian Modeling Signal Detection Confidence & Metacognition
🌀

4. Hypnosis & Cognitive Penetration

Hypnosis can induce striking alterations in sensation, perception, and cognition—allowing individuals to override highly automated information processing routines. In this research line, we investigate the exact cortical hierarchy at which hypnotic suggestions modulate neural sensory processing, probing the limits of cognitive penetrability.

Hypnosis Top-down Control Sensory Modulation
💬

5. Consciousness and Language Processing

What is the relationship between conscious access and linguistic integration? To what extent can semantic analysis and syntax processing occur in the complete absence of conscious awareness? We address these questions using masked priming and time-frequency EEG analysis to understand the boundaries of unconscious language comprehension.

Semantic Processing Masked Priming EEG Time-Frequency
💊

6. Neuropharmacology of Conscious Experience (ERC Studies)

Most of our ERC-funded research programs combine targeted pharmacological challenges (e.g. GABAergic, NMDA, cholinergic receptor modulation) with simultaneous neuroimaging to uncover the causal neurotransmitter systems that underpin conscious perception and cognitive flexibility.
