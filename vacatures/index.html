Open Positions / Vacatures

We are always interested in hearing from enthusiastic PhD candidates, postdocs, and master/bachelor students passionate about consciousness research.

PhD Candidates & Postdoctoral Fellowships

Open for Inquiries

Funded PhD and Postdoc positions funded through ERC and NWO grants are announced periodically on academic job portals and on this page. If you hold an independent fellowship (e.g., Marie Skłodowska-Curie, EMBO, NWO Rubicon/Veni) or are planning to apply for one, please get in touch with one of the PIs.

Master & Bachelor Student Internships

Brain & Cognitive Sciences / Psychology

We regularly supervise Research Master and Bachelor thesis projects (e.g. Brain & Cognitive Sciences at UvA or VU). Projects typically involve hands-on EEG or psychophysics data collection, preprocessing, and multivariate analysis.
